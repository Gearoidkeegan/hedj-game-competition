## Approved design (user, 2026-11-09)
1. **Cash clarity.**
   - The cash meter drops as soon as an add-on is picked; un-picking it restores the cash.
   - The result screen shows a cash breakdown that adds up exactly: operations, the market move on exposures, hedges, add-ons.
   - Each exposure line says how realised volume compared with forecast.
   - Cards show the forecast range, which Cash Forecasting visibly narrows.
   - A plain facts line opens every result: the policy status, plus the main figure.
2. **Detailed charts (approach A).**
   - Real **weekly** FX and commodity prices from the same free public sources (ECB and Yahoo), in chart-only files loaded after start.
   - The quarter's weekly line is pinned to the engine's start and end values, so the chart and the result % always agree.
   - Taller charts with a % scale relative to the quarter start, month markers, and the hedged vs unhedged outcome shown.
   - Interest rates stay monthly.
   - Still no dates, years or price levels anywhere.
3. **Feedback that matches the result.**
   - Quips, board lines and chairman lines are chosen from the real outcome: policy status (in, over-hedged, under-hedged, wrong focus, none, adopted), whether the quarter was good or bad, and whether the hedge won or lost.
   - "Calm" is used only for a genuinely small quarter.
   - The chairman never talks over a policy breach.
   - Each chairman gets **at least 8 positive and 8 negative** lines in that caricature's voice, plus off-policy lines. No line repeats within a run.
   - The **Pro parody names** are used everywhere: J.J. Jameson, ElonUsk, Michael O'Scary, Jamie Diamond, Warren Biscuit, Steve Jobsworth, Jack Doorstep, Bill Hackman.
   - An anti-AI-style copy pass covers all player-facing text, using the checklist at `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-09-v32-feedback/anti-ai-checklist.md`.
4. **Out of scope:** the backend, scoring weights and `balance.js` numbers, Pro mechanics. Pro *copy* is in scope for the copy pass and the names.

## Review Focus
1. **The cash breakdown must equal the change in cash exactly,** to the cent, in every case: add-ons bought, a Cash Visibility refund, an event that scales notionals, bankruptcy.
2. **Weekly data with gaps or a late start** (dairy, copper, BRL), or weekly data not yet loaded: the chart falls back to the monthly series, with no NaN and no throw.
3. **Feedback consistency.** No result shows text that contradicts its facts line. For example, a "calm" quip next to a ±2% of starting cash move, or a "lucky" line on an in-policy quarter.
4. **No line repeats within a run, and it survives RESUME.** The same quarter shows the same line after a reload.
5. **Copy length and escaping** for every new line, including the 128+ chairman lines.

### Task 1: Cash clarity (worktree `hgc-wt-v32-cash`, branch `v32-cash`)

**Files:**
- `js/engine/sim/SimEngine.js` (summary fields only; no change to cash maths);
- `js/screens/simple/QuarterScreen.js`, `js/screens/simple/ResultScreen.js`;
- `js/ui/labels.js`, `js/ui/CashMeter.js` if needed;
- CSS;

**Engine:** the summary gains the parts of the cash change. Keep the cash maths itself unchanged:
- `ocf`: the operating cash flow;
- `exposureTotal`: Σ exposureImpact;
- `hedgeTotal`: Σ hedgeImpact;
- `addOnCost`: the power-up cost, net of any Cash Visibility cash;
- `cashBefore`.

Add a test that `cashBefore + ocf + exposureTotal + hedgeTotal − addOnCost === cashAfter` (within 1e-6) across many seeds and decisions. Old summaries without these fields must still render, with the breakdown hidden.

**Quarter screen:**
- **Picking a power-up:** the meter animates to the cash after the cost and shows a `−€3m`-style tag. Un-picking it animates the meter back.
- **Forecast range:** each fx and commodity card shows `Forecast ±25%`, taken from the engine's `forecastVariance(run)`. It updates the moment Cash Forecasting is picked (preview) or owned. Interest-rate cards show no range, because principal is contractual.

**Result screen, top to bottom:**
1. **A facts line**, for example:
   - `In policy: 62% of risk covered (board wants 50–75%)`
   - `Off policy: 100% hedged, board wants 50–75%`
   - `Off policy: big exposure under-hedged`
   - `No board policy yet`
   - `Policy adopted from next quarter`

   Use the engine's `policyCover` and `coverPercent` helpers from v3.1.
2. **The cash breakdown:** `Operations +$28m · Market +$12m · Hedges −$14m · Add-ons −$3m`, followed by `Cash +$23m`.
   - Show signed values with `formatCash`.
   - Drop zero parts, except Operations.
   - Wrap cleanly at 360 px.
3. **On each exposure line**, add the volume result: `Volume 9% under forecast` or `9% over`, or `Volume on forecast` when the miss is under 2%. When `hedgeRatio × forecast > realised`, add `(over-hedged)`.

### Task 2: Detailed charts with weekly data (worktree `hgc-wt-v32-charts`, branch `v32-charts`)

**Files:**
- Extend `tools/fetch-market-data.cjs` with a `--weekly` mode, or add `tools/fetch-weekly-data.cjs`.
- Create `data/market/fx-weekly.json` and `data/market/commodities-weekly.json`.
- `js/engine/MarketEngine.js`: an optional, lazy, chart-only loader.
- `js/ui/PriceChart.js`, `ResultScreen.js` and `QuarterScreen.js` (the chart parts only).
- CSS and tests.

**Data:**
- Fetch weekly closes for every FX pair and commodity in the monthly files:
  - FX: ECB daily rates, sampled at each week's last trading day;
  - commodities: Yahoo, `1wk` interval;
  - cover the same date ranges as the monthly files.
- Use a compact format, for example per series `{ "start": "YYYY-MM-DD", "v": [..] }`, one value per week with `null` for gaps. Round values to 5 significant figures.
- **Size budget:** at most 300 KB raw across both files.
- Loading starts after the title screen renders (not blocking). Charts use weekly data when it is loaded, and fall back to monthly otherwise.
- The engine never reads weekly data.
- **If network access to ECB or Yahoo is blocked in your environment:** finish the fetcher and the loader with fixture-based tests, then stop with status NEEDS_CONTEXT. The controller will run the fetch.

**Chart:**
- **Quarter window:** from the engine's start-rate date to its end-rate date. Monthly keys are the first day of each quarter month (see `MarketEngine.getRate`).
- **Pinning:** add a linear correction across the window so the first and last points equal the engine's start and end rates. Use the same pinning on the trailing history so the join is seamless.
- **Height:** about 110 px for up to 3 lines, about 80 px for more.
- **Faint % gridlines** at sensible steps (e.g. ±5% / ±10%), labelled only in %.
- **Three month markers** across the quarter; no month names.
- **Hedged vs unhedged:**
  - keep the gold protected area;
  - add a small two-value caption, e.g. `Unhedged +$12m · With hedge −$2m`;
  - keep it under 90 characters.
- **Quarter-screen sparkline:** weekly history too, never after the quarter start.
- Keep the v3.1 behaviour:
  - one shared ticker;
  - tap to skip;
  - reduced motion;
  - integer-crisp canvases;
  - wait for the charts before the board reveal.

### Task 3: Feedback that matches the result; chairman voices; names; copy pass (main checkout, after the merge)

**Files:**

- `js/engine/sim/contract.md`;

**Selection rules:**
- **`outcomeFor(summary, startingCash)`** returns:
  - `policyState`: `adopted` | `wrongFocus` | `over` | `under` | `in` | `none`, where over and under compare the cover with the band;
  - `quarter`: `good` | `bad` | `flat`, based on the market result (exposures plus hedges) as a share of starting cash, with `flat` meaning a magnitude under 0.5%;
  - `hedge`: `won` | `lost` | `none`.
- **Result quip pools:** keyed by the outcome, with `calm` only when `quarter === 'flat'` and no hedge result over 0.5%.
- **Board pools:**
  - split `luckyBreach` into `overLucky` and `underLucky`, and `breachLoss` into `overLoss` and `underLoss`;
  - the copy names the breach ("you went past the board's limit", "you hedged less than the board asked for");
  - keep the other keys, but check each one's copy fits its trigger.
- **Chairman:**
  - pools per chairman: `positive` (at least 8), `negative` (at least 8), `offPolicy` (at least 6);
  - each line is 90 characters or fewer, in the caricature's voice; Pro's `data/board-dialogue.json` `ceo_special` lines are a source to adapt;
  - picked by outcome;
  - **the chairman yields** to `adopted`, `wrongFocus` and every over or under breach line, unless it is speaking an `offPolicy` line.
- **No repeats:** no line repeats within a run, and the result is resume-stable. Derive it deterministically from the run seed, the pool key and how many earlier quarters used that pool; for example, a seeded permutation indexed by use count.
- **Names:** `CHAIRMAN_SPRITES` labels become the Pro names. Grep for any leftover "The Editor" and similar.

**Copy pass:**
- Follow `anti-ai-checklist.md`. Every rewritten line keeps its meaning, its tone and its length limit.
- Don't touch code comments or identifiers.
